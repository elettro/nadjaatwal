About Nadja Atwal

Media, business, and innovation at global scale.

Nadja Atwal is a Manhattan-based, award-winning media architect, PR strategist, and business advisor operating at the intersection of media, capital, and emerging technology.

As a trusted Chief Advisor to U.S. and international companies, she works closely with founders and leadership teams on market entry, brand authority, and strategic expansion—often with aligned equity participation.

Her work is rooted in real business arenas, from the NYSE floor to global forums such as CES and Davos, where she interviews CEOs, investors, and policymakers and translates high-stakes conversations into clear public narratives.

Nadja hosts a top-ranking U.S. podcast with highlights airing on CNBC and serves on the leadership board of the Global AI Council, while actively supporting AI and deep-tech ventures shaping the next decade.

Originally a journalist and foreign correspondent for major German media outlets, she now helps executives become recognized thought leaders—building visibility, credibility, and high-value relationships that accelerate growth.
