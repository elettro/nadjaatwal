About Nadja Atwal

Media, business, and innovation at global scale.

Nadja Atwal is a Manhattan-based award-winning media architect, PR strategist, and business advisor recognized for helping companies accelerate growth through strategic positioning, high-level access, and narrative control. She operates at the intersection of media, business, and emerging technologies.

She serves as a trusted advisor and Chief Advisor to multiple U.S. and international companies, often working closely with leadership teams on market entry, brand authority, strategic expansion, and executive visibility.

A sought-after speaker, moderator, and panel host, Nadja is regularly featured in conversations about media influence, public perception, AI, technology, leadership, and business culture.

Originally starting her career as a journalist and foreign correspondent for major German media outlets, Nadja evolved into a high-impact operator and entrepreneur combining media influence with hands-on execution and strategic insight.
